How it works

Live video cannot wait for retransmissions. Phantom Frames never asks for a lost packet again; the receiver synthesises what is missing before the 100 ms playout deadline.

Tile

Each frame becomes 40×40 JPEG tiles: 144 at 360p, 576 at 720p.

Packetise

Tiles are interleaved into RTP packets of at most 1200 bytes, so a burst of loss scatters.

Lose

The channel drops packets at random or in Gilbert–Elliott bursts. Nothing is resent.

Detect

At the deadline the receiver knows exactly which tiles never arrived.

Repair

RepairNet, a 1.9 M-parameter U-Net, fills only those tiles from motion and their neighbours.

Measured results

Offline evaluation on held-out clips: people, motion, sports, film and games, with the same losses for every scheme.
